# Authorized business reports and durable electronic receipt delivery

Task #4 adds role-scoped operational reports, chart and XLSX presentation, and an asynchronous email receipt pipeline shared by seller, manager, and superadmin workflows. Reporting stays behind authorization-aware database functions, while receipt requests persist an immutable safe snapshot and use a leased outbox worker for idempotent delivery. The final visualization correction prevents P&L accounting values from being represented as pie percentages without removing valid pie charts from other reports. Builds and focused smoke checks pass; SQL runtime execution could not be repeated because local PostgreSQL is unavailable.

## High-level view

Security-definer report RPCs retain the existing global-versus-branch authorization gate. The browser derives charts and typed, formula-safe XLSX output from those report rows; P&L now opts out of pie rendering while other report types retain it.

Receipt requests capture a permission-checked, limited sale projection in an immutable outbox. Leased workers use stable provider idempotency keys and preserve ambiguous outcomes instead of risking duplicate sends.

Seller receipts use an explicitly entered destination, while delivered orders use the stored customer email. Seller, manager, and superadmin surfaces share the same delivery lifecycle and only issue a fresh idempotency key for a deliberate retry after terminal failure.

## Report authorization and the P&L chart boundary

The seller, product, and inventory-count reports call `require_report_access`, preserving admin-only global reads and authorized manager/accountant branch reads. Updated sales and P&L calculations account for quantity, void sales, refunded pre-tax revenue, and recovered cost for resellable returns. XLSX text is guarded against formula interpretation while numeric and date cells remain typed.

The prior chart defect is resolved in two independent places. `buildReportVisualization('profit', ...)` returns `pieAvailable: false`, disabling the tab, and `ChartView` rejects direct P&L pie rendering. The focused smoke check also confirms ordinary sales data still produces an enabled, non-empty pie breakdown.

## Immutable receipt snapshots and worker leases

`get_safe_sale_receipt` omits customer contact details, cost, profit, and unrelated order data, and checks sale actor, branch-management, or owning-customer access. Queue creation normalizes the destination, binds the semantic request and actor to an idempotency key, and rejects conflicting reuse. The outbox guard prevents later recipient or snapshot changes.

Workers claim rows with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, lock tokens, and stale-lease recovery. Completion requires the matching lease and caps retries; uncertain provider responses become `ambiguous`, preserving the intended at-most-once posture.

## Receipt trust boundaries and message safety

Raw outbox access and claim/finish RPCs remain service-role-only, while authenticated queue operations enforce sale ownership or management authorization and require delivered orders with a customer email. The dispatch endpoint validates its bearer cron secret with `timingSafeEqual` before creating the service client. Receipt-derived HTML is escaped, recipient and subject header injection is blocked, remote logos require HTTPS, and status results expose only a masked recipient hint.
