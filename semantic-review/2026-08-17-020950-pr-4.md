# Authorized reports and durable electronic receipt delivery

Task #4 adds authorized operational reports, XLSX/chart presentation, sanitized receipt snapshots, and a durable Resend-backed delivery outbox used by seller, manager, and admin workflows. The follow-up separates staff-only sending from customer receipt reads, requires an active profile for status access, and preserves accepted-but-not-finalized sends for same-key recovery. Accounting now excludes void sales, uses tax-exclusive revenue/refunds, and emits daily seller/product rows; aggregate reports now receive categorical chart data. **Watch for: confirmed — the P&L fallback is exposed as a pie chart even though its metrics overlap and are not parts of one total, making its percentages mathematically meaningless.**

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Receipt reading and sending now have distinct trust boundaries: customers retain access to their own safe receipt, while only the originating seller or authorized management can enqueue delivery. Status resolution rejects inactive profiles.

The outbox snapshots receipt content and combines expiring leases with a stable provider key. Provider submission and database finalization are separate, so an accepted send whose finalization fails remains `processing` for same-key recovery; uncertain submissions stop in `ambiguous`.

Reports use existing global/branch authorization and consistent business-day ranges. Sales and performance outputs exclude void transactions, while P&L recognizes line revenue and refunds without tax and restores COGS only for resellable returns.

Aggregate fallback fixes empty charts, but the P&L UI treats revenue, deductions, subtotals, and final profit as slices of one pie. The underlying values are coherent; the displayed proportions are not.

## Receipt authorization and delivery recovery are now separate concerns

`queue_sale_receipt_delivery` independently requires the sale’s active seller, a branch manager, or a global admin; customer access through `can_access_safe_sale_receipt` no longer grants sender authority. Order delivery remains management-only and takes its destination from the delivered order. `get_receipt_delivery_status` requires an active profile and returns only a masked recipient hint.

The worker submits with `receipt-<idempotency_key>` before calling `finish_receipt_delivery`. A finalization failure leaves the lease in `processing`, allowing stale-lease reclamation with the same provider key. Network uncertainty and a 2xx response without a provider ID become terminal `ambiguous` states, while explicit transient rejection follows bounded retries. Immutable snapshot fields and lease-token matching prevent recipient/payload changes or stale-worker finalization.

The email renderer escapes receipt-controlled HTML, rejects header-breaking recipient input, permits only HTTPS logo URLs, and strips subject newlines. Authenticated clients receive RPC access rather than direct outbox-table access.

## P&L and daily performance share the corrected accounting basis

`report_profit_loss` derives revenue from line totals less tax, prorates completed refunds to their tax-exclusive component, and restores COGS only for resellable inventory. Void sales are excluded from revenue and COGS. Seller and product reports group by Tashkent business date and entity, while the sales export also excludes void rows and multiplies cost by quantity. The report functions call `require_report_access`, limiting global data to global admins and branch data to global admins or that branch’s manager/accountant.

## P&L pie percentages combine overlapping metrics

**Confirmed:** `buildReportVisualization('profit', ...)` places `sales_revenue`, `refunds`, `net_sales`, `cost_of_goods`, `gross_profit`, `other_income`, `operating_expenses`, and `net_profit` in one categorical series. `ChartView` supplies their absolute values to a pie labeled as group shares. Because `net_sales` already contains revenue less refunds, `gross_profit` contains net sales less COGS, and `net_profit` contains gross profit plus/minus the remaining items, the slices repeatedly count the same money and have no meaningful total.

Keep P&L as a bar comparison, waterfall, or statement chart, but disable its pie mode unless the source becomes mutually exclusive. A chart-capability flag from `buildReportVisualization` would keep invalid modes out of the UI.
